WavePool privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

WavePool is a synthesizer app that turns short recordings into instruments. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing about you.

What WavePool does not do

WavePool has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not send anything to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no code from third parties that collects data.

The microphone

WavePool asks for microphone access so you can record sounds to play. A recording is made only when you touch the Record control and ends when you stop it, or after ten seconds. WavePool never listens in the background and never records without you starting it.

What is stored on your device

Your recordings, your saved sounds with their names and settings, and your last session are stored in the app's private storage on your own iPhone or iPad. They stay there. They are not uploaded. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, Apple's backup may include this data, under your own Apple settings. Deleting WavePool deletes it.

Exporting and sharing

If you use Export wavetable, WavePool makes a file and shows the standard iOS share sheet. What happens next is your choice, for example saving it to Files or sending it to another app. WavePool does not see or control where it goes.

MIDI

WavePool can receive notes and controller messages from MIDI devices you connect to your device. They are used to play sound and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Because WavePool collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds an online feature, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
